About

Our story, our passion, our coffee

Our Story

Brew & Bean was founded in 2015 with a simple mission: to serve the best coffee in Karachi while creating a warm, welcoming space for our community. What started as a small corner cafe has grown into a beloved local spot where people come to connect, work, and enjoy exceptional coffee.

Our founder, Ali Khan, traveled across South America and Africa to source the finest coffee beans, bringing back not just beans, but stories and relationships with farmers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.

Our Coffee

We're passionate about every step of the coffee journey—from seed to cup. We work directly with farmers who practice sustainable agriculture, ensuring fair prices and supporting local communities.

Our beans are roasted in small batches to bring out their unique flavors, and our baristas are trained to craft each drink with precision and care. Whether you prefer a strong espresso, a creamy latte, or a refreshing cold brew, we have something to delight your palate.

Meet Our Baristas

Why Choose Us

At Brew & Bean, we believe coffee is more than just a drink—it's an experience. That's why we focus on:

  • Quality: We use only the finest, ethically sourced beans
  • Craftsmanship: Every drink is carefully prepared by trained baristas
  • Community: We're proud to be a gathering place for our neighborhood
  • Sustainability: We're committed to eco-friendly practices, from compostable cups to energy-efficient equipment
